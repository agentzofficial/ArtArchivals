**agent z Art Contest** is the first competition held by agent z in celebration of 10,000 subscribers. It predates the *agent z Versus* branding. It was held on a limited time Discord Server that opened prior on the 12th of June 2025.

The contest officially began when the Splatoon Animation "Beach Episode" was released and ended when the agent z Art Contest Discord Server was officially archived.

<h2 id="announcement">Announcement</h2>
The existance of a 10,000 subscriber special was first teased in a Bluesky post on the 25th of May 2025, with a render displaying Agent Z drawing something on a piece of paper with paintbrushes, with a cube with the Discord logo a distance away. The same render would be posted on Twitter on the 2nd of June 2025.

An official Discord server then titled the "agent z Discord Server" would be made available in this community post. The art contest hadn't been announced, being masked as a "special event" to be announced. <h2 id="guidelines_to_participate">Guidelines to Participate</h2>
ANYONE may participate, whether you're subscribed or not! For your art submission to be valid though, your piece must follow these guidelines:
- Art must feature the original characters Agent Z OR Agent X in a reasonable capacity. It can't look like they were added last second for the sake of the contest; You can feature your own or other characters too.
- Art must be owned by, and hence made by mostly you. No edits from existing artwork if you did not make them or images made by Artificial Intelligence.
- No NSFW/innappropiate art submissions or art submissions with political or controversial topics.

BONUS: If your art piece features a reference of sorts to an animation on the agent z YouTube channel, you get +2 points from me for Round 1;

All software styles are allowed, whether its a digital drawing, drawing on paper, Garry's Mod, Source Filmmaker, Blender, anything!

<h3 id="how_to_submit_artwork">How to Submit Artwork</h3>
You can submit your artwork any way you like, as long as I'm able to see it and you make it clear its for the contest. Imgur via a link,
Twitter, Discord, Bluesky, etc. If it is not through Discord, please @ me to ensure I see your contest submission.

Please include the following in your submission:
- Title: (your artwork's title)
- Social: (ONE social that defines the "author" of your art, e.g, "by @...". If none is given, the account used to submit the artwork will be used.)
These details will be present during voting rounds.

<h3 id="prize_sheet">Prize Sheet</h3>
Note: Due to currency differences, I will do my best to send the amount as close as possible to the USD values. This may result in you receiving slightly more than intended.
If you want a non-Splatoon Original Character to represent you, please let me know immediately and I'll get into talks with you if it's possible (I'm limited to Garry's Mod!)

<h3 id="disclaimers">Disclaimers!!!</h3>
By submitting artwork, you acknowledge that you are participating in a **CONTEST**, where your artwork will be put against others for people to see and make votes on. You may still face disappointment in results or unexpected reception for your art pieces. With that in mind, please avoid starting personal drama or complaining publicly in these regards. All submissions will also be posted to YouTube and other agent z Social Medias when announcing the formal end to the contest.
I am new to doing this, so please be patient with me if things get shaky!

To receive your prizes, you must have one of the following relative to the prize you want:
- A Steam account
- A Nintendo account, where you must be willing to provide your account's location/region details because Nintendo eShop gift cards are region locked.
- A Discord account

Note: Artwork credits use the username data accurate at the time of the agent z Art Contest. If you are an artist credited and see outdated data that you wish to update, please contact agent z via social media platforms.

<h2 id="aftermath">Aftermath</h2>
The 10,000 Subscriber Render would take longer than expected to be completed. As the Voting Rounds for the agent z Art Contest ended on the 26th of July 2025 and to not prolong the server's lifetime, the server would be archived on the 30th of July 2025. Updates would continue being released in regards to the 10,000 Subscriber Render, as well as the release of the Squid Game episode.
<img class="zoom-trigger" data-id="001-002" src="/assets/images/2025-08-14_1.png" alt="preview">

On the 14th of August 2025, the 10,000 Subscriber Render was released, with a discussion thread on the agent z Art Contest Discord Server being opened one hour prior to the 10,000 Subscriber Render's release. The disccussion thread remained open until the 16th of August 2025, when it was closed, leaving the agent z Art Contest Discord Server completely archived until a new event is hosted by agent z.

Leading up to the the discussion thread closing, agent z released two zip files containing all of the agent z Art Contest Discord Server's emojis for preservation in case the emojis are removed in future events to make room for new emojis. A collage of all of the art submissions for the agent z Art Contest would be posted at the same time of the discussion thread closing, marking the official end of the agent z Art Contest.
