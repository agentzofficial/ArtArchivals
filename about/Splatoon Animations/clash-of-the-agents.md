  **Clash of the Agents** is the seventh episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It is also the sixth official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 18th of May, 2024.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agent X grows closer to her goals... can Agent 3 and Agent 8 stop her before it is too late?!"

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode is a direct continuation of the previous episode, [Price for Power](/about/price-for-power). It also marks the first use of the weapon Agent X had been developing. Neo Agent 3 also makes a direct reference to [Fireworks Festival](/about/fireworks-festival), saying it was the point in which Agent Z stopped messaging her.

  This episode also marks the first appearance of Agent Z and Agent X's backstories, being teased as motivation for Agent Z.

  In the scene with Agent Z inside her computer, she is surrounded by several renders referencing **agent z Classics** that Agent X and her had appeared in.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - Neo Agent 3 mentions having Splashdown in this episode and later uses it in [Battle of Inkopolis Square](/about/battle-of-inkopolis-square).
  - The 4,000 Subscriber Render that was released 4 days after this episode's release is themed around this episode.
  - Octavio's observation on Agent X's plan is confirmed in [Battle of Inkopolis Square](/about/battle-of-inkopolis-square).
  - Several minor characters that appear in this episode would later appear in [Hospital Buddies](/about/hospital-buddies), due to events that would take place after this episode.
  - Octavio's destroyed airship reappears in [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents) in a comedic skit.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - Agent 8 with Inkjet is a reference to that special being prominant in Octo Expansion, particularly during the Diaphragm Phase in the Deepsea Metro Escape.
  - Neo Agent 3 with Splashdown is a reference to that special being her main special in Return of the Mammalians.
  - This episode takes place during the first Friends vs. Family vs. Solo Splatfest back on the 13th of January 2024, 4 months prior to this episode's release. This Splatfest was chosen as this episode takes place at least a few days after [Fireworks Festival](/about/fireworks-festival) on New Year's Day, as shown in [Price for Power](/about/price-for-power).
  - At the time of this episode canonically taking place, Inkopolis Square would've been inaccessible to the player as Side Order released on the 22nd of February 2024.
  - Inside of Agent Z's computer saw 13 renders referencing several **agent z Classics**, while Agent Z's quick flashback teasing her backstory contains 14 renders. This equals to 27 renders made that are viewable in full quality via [agent z Art Archivals](/artarchivals).
  - This episode marks the first instance of the outro being altered, lacking music and having less saturation.
