  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  Neo Agent 3 travels through underground tunnels, not realising it's been occupied by an unknown beast.

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode follows off of the Power Trip Story Arc and the destruction of Inkopolis Square, showing that it is still undergoing repairs since the location's last appearance in [Power Outage](about/power-outage). It also explains what remains inside the drain in Inkopolis Square, having been blocked off by tires for a majority of the series as far back as the agent z Classics series of videos.

  This episode also presumably takes place before [Lost Phone](/about/lost-phone), as at the end, Agent X states that she will get a soda, which she later asks Agent Z if she wants one in that episode.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This is the first episode to feature Agent X in the thumbnail since 'Stories of the Agents', back in September 2024, 581 days ago and excluding thumbnails from agent z YouTube Memberships.
  - This episode marks Off the Hook's first appearance in 2026, leaving Deep Cut as the only idols that have yet to make an appearance in 2026.
