  **Hospital Buddies** is the tenth episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the final episode released in 2024.

  It was released on the 8th of December, 2024.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agents 3 and 8 must wait inside a hospital... with Neo Agent 3."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This is the first episode released after the Power Trip Story Arc and aimed to steer the series back to a comedic tone while giving further closure to the events of [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents).

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - The book that Agent 8 is reading in this episode has reappeared in future episodes, such as being read by an inkling in [Power Outage](/about/power-outage) and by Agent 3 inside of Neo Agent 3's imagination in [Library Thief](/about/library-thief).

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - Many of the extra inkling characters that appear in the hospital had formerly appeared in [Clash of the Agents](/about/clash-of-the-agents), implying that they are also in the hospital due to the events of the Power Trip Story Arc.
  - This episode notably does not feature Agent Z, though she is mentioned by Marie.
  - This episode was the last episode released in 2024, though [The Perfect Present](/about/the-perfect-present) was originally supposed to be the final episode for 2024, but it was delayed to as far as March 2025.
