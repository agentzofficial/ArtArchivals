  **Fireworks Festival** is the fourth episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the last episode released in 2023. It is also the first Fan Character video, featuring original characters submitted by fans. This episode would see 57 fan characters featured.

  It is also the third official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 31st of December, 2023.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "A new year's festival involving fireworks is being hosted, and YOU'RE invited!! Now you get to see Agent 3 trying to prevent Neo Agent 3 from causing chaos in real time."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  The end of the episode teases at the Power Trip Story Arc, with Agent X confirmed to be ready to execute a plan she has. Her plan would be seen in the next episode.

  This episode also debutted Fan Character videos, special episodes that allowed fans to submit their original characters via a YouTube Community Post, or via a post on Twitter. This format would later be used again the next year in 2024 with The Perfect Present.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - Several characters from this episode reappear in The Perfect Present.
  - In the 10,000 Subscriber Render, Agent 3 can be seen interacting with the same Fan Character who had helped Agent 8 look for her previously in this episode.
  - Production of Fireworks Festival is discussed in a Retrospective video on The Perfect Present. This video is exclusive under an agent z YouTube Membership.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The font coloring for each Fan Character followed a consistent format, with the Fan Character's team ink color being the font's main color while their eye color would be the font outline's color. Characters with overlapping or similar color schemes would have shading adjusted.
