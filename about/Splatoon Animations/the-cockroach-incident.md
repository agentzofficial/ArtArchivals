  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  A cockroach invades Agent Z's room, and she wants it GONE.

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode acts as a spiritual succcessor to [The SPIDER](/about/the-spider), featuring a bug who enters a character's home, causing panic.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This episode notably does not feature Agent 3, Agent 8, and Neo Agent 3, and is the first episode to not feature any of the three.
  - This is the first episode to be entirely located in Agent X's house.
  - Octavio's Headquarters makes its first apperance since [Clash of the Agents](/about/clash-of-the-agents), two years prior.
  - Likewise with [The SPIDER](/about/the-spider), this episode is based on personal experiences with cockroaches by agent z.
