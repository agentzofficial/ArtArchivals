  **Cooking Together** is the eighteenth episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the first episode released in 2026.

  It was released on the 1st of February, 2026.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agent 3 tries to cook herself some food to eat. What could go wrong?"

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  The episode sees the following references in future episodes:
  - [Celebrity Crush](/about/celebrity-crush) – Agent 3 can be seen riding a bike, referencing a joke in this episode.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This episode marks the earliest release of the first episode in a year, with 2024's first episode [Agent's Infiltrating Mission](/about/agents-infiltrating-mission) releasing on the 11th of February and 2025's first episode [The Perfect Present](/about/the-perfect-present) releasing on the 30th of March.
  - This is the first episode to not feature Neo Agent 3 since [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents) and the first to not feature Neo Agent 3 in any physical capacity.
  - This is also the first episode to be under 10 minutes since the very first episode in the Splatoon Animation series, [Agent X Tries YOUR Weapons](/about/agent-x-tries-your-weapons).
  - This episode also marks a slight change in the outro, now featuring diagonal bars for the background.
