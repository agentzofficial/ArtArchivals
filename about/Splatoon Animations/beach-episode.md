  **Beach Episode** is the thirteenth episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It was released on the 22nd of June, 2023.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "the Agents all go to the beach. it happens at least once in every animated series."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode also announced the agent z Art Contest, in celebration of 10,000 subscribers. It marks the first promotional segment and voice acting in an episode, seen at the beginning to announce the agent z Art Contest and would be seen again, primarily in the agent z YouTube Membership Overview Trailer.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - The 10,000 Subscriber Render makes reference to this episode with Neo Agent 3 in the helicopter.
  - Two jellyfish in a skit about sharing chips are referenced when their posts are shown by Agent X to Agent 3 in [The Countdown](/about/the-countdown).

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - An aim for this episode was to feature as many Splatoon characters as possible, and thus many Splatoon characters make their debut in this episode.
  - All of the Idols also make an appearance in this episode, with each character having a small scene to themselves.
