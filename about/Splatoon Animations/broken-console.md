  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  One of Agent Z's most valued treasures, the Nintendo Switch 2... is BROKEN!?

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode announces a special project from agent z, being a VTuber model, which the episode itself is centered around.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - Agent Z watching a live stream earlier into the episode foreshadows the ending to the episode.
  - This is the third episode in 2026 to not feature Neo Agent 3, and the second to not feature any of the canon characters, including Agent 3 and Agent 8. The first episode was [The Cockroach Incident](/about/the-cockroach-incident).
