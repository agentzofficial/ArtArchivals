  It was released on the 3rd of December, 2023.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Neo Agent 3 wants to join the pretty cool circus, and she have just the tricks to get herself in..."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  The episode makes a direct reference to a Splatshort that had been posted a few weeks prior with a skit between Callie and Marie. It also makes a direct reference to a video featuring countryballs, being the first video uploaded on a seperate YouTube Channel that agent z had formerly worked on.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - The same door that appears throughout the episode has appeared in several renders.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The episode was originally named "THE AMAZING DIGITAL DOOR", but was changed to be more simplified and neutral.
  - This is the first episode to not be officially part of a Story Arc.
  - This is technically the first crossover Splatoon Animation.
