  **Battle of Inkopolis Square** is the eighth episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It is also the seventh official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 1st of September, 2024.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agent X has made it to Inkopolis Square! Agent 3 and Neo Agent 3 rush over to force her away, only to find something terrible..."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode is a direct continuation of [Clash of the Agents](/about/clash-of-the-agents). It notably marks the confrontation with Agent 3 and co. against Agent X and  that Agent Z is independently planning her own weapon to counter Agent X. At the end of the episode, it is revealed that Inkopolis Square has been destroyed. As of now, it is still being rebuilt.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - Scenes from this episode were shown in a flashback sequence in [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents) and later by Agent 3 in [Squid Game](/about/squid-game).
  - Agent Z and Agent X's backstory, first shown in this episode, was expanded further in the next episode, [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents).
  - A different, more complete version of the music track Agent X made and showed Agent Z is played in [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents).

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The intro is unique in that it has a fade-out effect.
  - This and the next episode, [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents), were intended to be one large episode to act as the finale to the Power Trip Story Arc, but they were split into two due to prolonged delays. The next episode would release at the end of the month, as stated at the end of this episode.
  - This is the first episode to be over 20 minutes long, and would be the longest episode on the agent z YouTube Channel at 28 minutes 42 seconds until the next episode, [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents), surpassed it, being 7 minutes 18 seconds longer at 36 minutes long.
  - Assuming the episode lengths remained the same, the finale would've been 1 hour, 4 minutes and 42 seconds long had the two remained one episode.
  - During flashback scenes, dialogue intentionally excludes the character's name to indicate who's speaking.
  - Agent 8 being brainwashed was not in the original script, but was added due to high speculation and expectations on her role in the finale to the Story Arc. Originally, she would've remained unconcious for the whole finale.
