  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This is the first episode to release without any delays in 2026 and since [The Countdown](/about/the-countdown) back on the 27th of December 2025.
  - Pearl hitting Neo Agent 3 with a kart is a reference to a tweet from Nintendo, stating that she has a kart. The tweet can be found here: https://x.com/NintendoAmerica/status/971098380926701568
  - An alternate thumbnail is visibly rushed as it was intended to be temporary until a better thumbnail could be made so the video would be released on time.
