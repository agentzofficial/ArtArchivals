  **Agent's Infiltrating Mission** is the fifth episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the first episode released in 2024.

  It is also the fourth official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 11st of February, 2024.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Octavio is planning his most evil plan yet, and Agent 3, Agent 8, and Neo Agent 3 are tasked to stop him! But things don't go according to plan..."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode is the last episode to release before the Power Trip Story Arc was officially announced in the following episode. Many hints are given to the villain of the Story Arc in this episode, and several scenes would be remade in a different perspective in [Price for Power](/about/price-for-power), which acts as a continuation of this episode.

  The music track that plays when Agent X appears in the episode, "Airship BGM – Super Mario Bros." from Super Mario Maker, is also referenced in future episodes in the Power Trip Story Arc, in remixed forms.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - This being the first instance of Agent X using the Octobot King machine, this is referenced in the 10,000 Subscriber Render. However, it can also be seen as a general reference to the Power Trip Story Arc.
  - While a very indirect reference, a character in the 10,000 Subscriber Render is seen using a green GameBoy, the same color scheme Neo Agent 3 can be seen playing in this episode.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This is the first episode to have a more serious tone throughout than the episodes before it.
