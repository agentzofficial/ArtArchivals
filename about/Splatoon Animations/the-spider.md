  **The SPIDER** is the second episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It is also the second official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 30th of October, 2023.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agent 8 and Neo Agent 3 try to get rid of a spider while Agent 3 is out. How hard can it be?"

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode features a scene of Agent X doing ongoing research on the most powerful weapon possible, being the reason why she doesn't appear to help Agent 8 and Neo Agent 3 with the spider.

  This episode is one of agent z's most popular in viewership, and likely solidified the decision to keep making 10 minute animations. As such it has also been referenced in future media:
  - The spider has reappeared in several renders and in other episodes, being [Squid Game](/about/squid-game) and [The Countdown](/about/the-countdown).

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This episode was inspired by agent z's own encounters with spiders.
  - This episode is technically a Halloween episode, as evident by a unique laughing sound in the intro.
  - This is the only episode so far with a unique sound for the intro.
  - As of January 2026, this is agent z's most viewed video.
