  **Enter Super Mario 64** is the sixteenth episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It was released on the 14th of December, 2025.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "The Agents cross over into an entirely different, polygon-y world."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This is the first episode to feature a character not from the Splatoon universe prominanently throughout the episode. This character being Mario from the Super Mario series. Other characters from the Super Mario series also appear, such as Bowser and Peach.

  This episode also ends the rivarly between Agent 3 and Agent X, who are now friends as seen in the next episode, [The Countdown](/about/the-countdown).

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - In [The Countdown](/about/the-countdown), when Agent X is playing on the Nintendo Switch 2 by herself, the game she is playing can be heard to be Super Mario 64.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The current thumbnail is a direct reference to the Super Mario 64 DS box art. An alternate thumbnail makes reference to the original Super Mario 64's box art.
  - This is the first instance of the outro music being different, playing "Staff Roll" from Super Mario 64 instead of the remix of "Blitz It!" from Splatoon 2.
  - Mario's design is seen to be intentionally altered to be more saturated and brighter.
