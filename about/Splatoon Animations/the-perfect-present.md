  **The Perfect Present** is the eleventh episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the first episode released in 2025. It is also the second Fan Character video, featuring original characters submitted by fans. This episode would see 147 fan characters features.

  It was released on the 30th of March, 2025.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Agent Z doesn't know what Agent X would want as a gift! She looks towards YOU for some inspiration..."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode recieved the longest delays out of any other video from its original announced release date of the 28th of December 2024 to its release on the 30th of March 2025, about a 3 month or a 92 day delay. A retrospective video exclusive to the agent z YouTube Membership goes into detail as to what exactly caused this long delay. It is notably the first episode to recieve a dedicated retrospective video.

  The "incoming call" interface is standardised in this episode as it is seen again in [Enter Super Mario 64](/about/enter-super-mario-64), being updated from an interface shown in [The SPIDER](/about/the-spider).

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - The city location Agent Z finds herself in after leaving the mall and inviting Agent X to a dinner is seen again in [Library Thief](/about/library-thief), showing that the library is in the same location.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This is the first episode outside of a Story Arc to not feature Agent 3 and Agent 8 in any physical capacity. Neo Agent 3 technically appears in a quick timeskipping scene. The retrospective explains the reason for this.
  - Several posters of different medias can be seen in the background in the mall. Notably, a Nintendo Switch 2 one can be seen, which was added later in production as the Nintendo Switch 2 was announced in January 2025, after this episode's original scheduled release in December 2024.
  - The horror movie being watched is a parody of "SMILE 2".
  - The scene involving the creation of a large burger is a reference to the "Burger Builders" minigame from "Super Mario Party Jamboree".
