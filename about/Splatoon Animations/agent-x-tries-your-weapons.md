  **Agent X Tries YOUR Weapons** is the first episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It is also the first official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 10th of September, 2023.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Earlier in a community post, I asked YOU for weapons for Agent X to use, let's see how they turned out..."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode unintentionally began the Power Trip Story Arc, acting as the catalyst to Agent X aiming to develop the strongest weapon possible. This plot point is solidified in the following episode, The SPIDER.

  This episode also established the fact that Agent X does not have a set main weapon. Scenes that feature Agent X needing to use a weapon have her scavenging her pocket with a slot machine-esque jingle to indicate that the weapon she uses are random and not her set main.

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - One of the last few scenes in this episode, featuring Agent Z looking through the mailbox to a lack of other suggestions for weapons, is made into a render that appears in Clash of the Agents.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The scene referencing Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door was made prior to the Nintendo Switch remake's official announcement in a Nintendo Direct that followed 4 days after this episode's release.
  - This episode was inspired by the fact that agent z genuinely couldn't find a main for Agent X to use.
