  **The Countdown** is the seventeenth episode of the Splatoon Animation series and the last episode released in 2025.

  It was released on the 27th of December, 2025.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Timing THAT song's beat drop at 12am on New Year's Day!"

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode has yet to receive any references in subsequent episodes.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - This is the first Christmas special to not be a Fan Character video.
  - This episode marks the shortest gap between episodes with an only 2 week wait since [Enter Super Mario 64](/about/enter-super-mario-64) released on the 14th of December.
