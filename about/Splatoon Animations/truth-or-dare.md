  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  The Agents appear to be a bit bored together. Looks like they've looked to YOU for a game of Truths and Dares!

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This is the first episode to feature fan submissions since [The Perfect Present](/about/the-perfect-present), with that episode's submissions being opened on the 25th of November 2024, over a year prior. This time it did not request Fan Characters but fan submissions for a truth or dare game, being similar to [Agent X Tries YOUR Weapons](/about/agent-x-tries-your-weapons) with the episode going through fan submissions.

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - A lot of scenes in this episode reference past episodes in the series, such as the same beach from [Beach Episode](/about/beach-episode) appearing.
  - This is the first episode since [Library Thief](/about/library-thief) to not have multiple thumbnails tested. This is because the episode saw rapid growth under the current thumbnail that was intended on being temporary, but was chosen to stay as the thumbnail.
  - The current thumbnail was meant to be temporary to get the episode released on time.
  - This episode is the first episode to focus on Agent W, the computer entity, since their role in the Power Trip Story Arc back in [Clash of the Agents](/clash-of-the-agents), 2 years prior.
  - This is also the first episode to feature Fuzzy Octolings from Splatoon 3's singleplayer campaign 'Return of the Mammalians'.
