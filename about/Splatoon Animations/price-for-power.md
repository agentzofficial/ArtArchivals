  **Price for Power** is the sixth episode of the Splatoon Animation series.

  It is also the fifth official episode of the Power Trip Story Arc. It was released on the 7th of April, 2024.

  <h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
  "Power comes with a price. It's a burden. It demands sacrifice."

  <h2 id="legacy">Legacy</h2>
  This episode was released alongside the official announcement of the Power Trip Story Arc, marking the true beginning for the Story Arc. As such, plot elements from this episode would be continued in following episodes.

  The first appearance of a place inside of Agent Z's computer, officially named "Agent W", occurs here. It notably features the important plot elements of the creation of Agent X's powerful weapon and the bomb placed inside of Ammo Knights in Inkopolis Plaza that would be brought up in [Stories of the Agents](/about/stories-of-the-agents).

  Other agent z Content would also reference this episode:
  - The place inside of Agent Z's computer was teased to be expanded on via teaser renders for the next episode, [Clash of the Agents](/about/clash-of-the-agents).

  <h2 id="trivia">Trivia</h2>
  - The first half of this episode takes place right after [Fireworks Festival](/about/fireworks-festival) and later before [Agent's Infiltrating Mission](/about/agents-infiltrating-mission), before continuing after the events of the last episode, [Agent's Infiltrating Mission](/about/agents-infiltrating-mission).
  - This is the first episode to be made on a newer computer, with posts from Twitter mentioning how agent z's computer from 2017 had broken down during the production of this episode.
  - This episode's description is a quote taken from video game designer Drew Karpyshyn.
